# Trademarks

This policy implements Numinia canon C-005 §7 for this repository. The
licences in this repository cover copyright only: neither MIT nor CC0 nor
any other licence used by Numen Games S.L. grants trademark rights.

## Reserved marks (C-005 §7)

The following are identifiers of origin and are excluded from every free
licence: **Numinia**, **Numen Games**, **Khepri**, the logo, the isotype,
and the signature wordmarks.

## Permitted

- Citing, linking to, or writing about the project.
- Stating factually that a product or workspace uses Numen assets or was
  generated with NWOS ("Powered by NWOS").

## Not permitted

- Using the marks as your own brand or product name.
- Suggesting sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement.
- Presenting a fork or derived work as official.

*You can copy the system; you cannot say you are Numen.*

## Status of the "NWOS" mark — proposed, not decided

C-005 §7 reserves Numinia, Numen Games and Khepri, but does not list
**NWOS** (Numen Workspace Operating System), even though the mark travels
into every generated client workspace (README credit, `web/index.html`
badge, the C-004 glossary entry).

Proposal for Oracle consensus — a canon change requires it, and this
document cannot decide it:

1. Add **NWOS** to the reserved identifiers in C-005 §7.
2. Keep the factual credit expressly permitted, so generated workspaces
   may state "Powered by NWOS" without needing a trademark licence.

Until the canon settles the question, the Permitted / Not permitted lists
above are applied to NWOS as interim guidance.
